## AC-01: countable replacement for the Zorn minimization

**Written lemma (classical, countably indexed).** Suppose the commutative monoid of tensor classes M has a supplied enumeration t_0,t_1,..., possibly with repetitions, and a covering relation as in prop_gauge_monoid. Let p0 be an upper functional with finite nonnegative real values. Define K as the set of q satisfying 0<=q(t)<=p0(t), q(1)=1, submultiplicativity, all finite-cover inequalities, and every triple (x,y,c) respected by p0, i.e. q(tx)<=c q(ty) for all t. K is nonempty (p0) and closed in the product of compact intervals: every condition involves finitely many real coordinates. The arbitrary intersection over triples remains closed.

Enumerated product compactness has a classical countable nested-box proof. For example, rescale each nonzero bounded coordinate to [0,1]; use dyadic cylinders fixing finitely many coordinates at each stage, refining the first n coordinates to mesh at most 2^-n. Given a family of closed subsets with the finite intersection property, at least one of the finitely many children preserves this property, by the finite union argument. Choose the least child in a specified finite order by excluded middle. The resulting nested cylinders have a unique coordinatewise limit. Every closed set in the family contains it, since each cylinder meets that set and the cylinders eventually fit any basic neighborhood. This construction uses recursion and specified finite decisions, not a selection from arbitrary nonempty sets. It provides the relevant countable product compactness in classical ZF with the enumeration given.

Let K_0=K. At step i take the minimum of the continuous projection q -> q(t_i) on K_i and define K_(i+1) as the closed set attaining that value. All these compact sets are nonempty, and their construction is uniquely determined. Their nested intersection is nonempty. If p is in the intersection and q in K has q<=p pointwise, induction on i shows q is in every K_i and q(t_i)=p(t_i). The enumeration implies equality. Thus p has exactly the minimality property of Paper.prop_gauge_minimal. Alternatively minimize the uniformly convergent positive weighted sum sum_i 2^(-i-1) q(t_i)/(1+p0(t_i)); any strict pointwise decrease strictly decreases it.

Now apply the existing radius and localization arguments to this minimal p; their mathematical conclusion is multiplicativity. This removes the explicit Zorn invocation for the countable-index lemma. Finite tensors over any explicitly enumerated countable field are countable: enumerate dimensions and coefficient lists, then take the image in tensor classes. In particular this applies to finite fields. It does not establish constructive computability of the minimizer: testing nonemptiness of the closed constraints is generally non-effective, and excluded middle is used. It does not settle arbitrary uncountable fields or eliminate Lean's Classical.choice from the remaining proof. Zorn's use by the published proof does not establish that the proposition implies full AC.

**Native gate:** implement a countable compactness/minimality lemma independent of the upstream proof, then substitute it into prop_gauge_monoid. Audit #print axioms of prop_gauge_minimal, radius, localize, prop_gauge, thm_duality, prop_space_midpoints, and thm_alt. Distinguish mathematical ZF argument from Lean foundational axiom reporting. Investigate transfer through an explicitly countable algebraic closure of the prime field; require a proof of transfer for each predicate. Do not assume countable field implies a finite tensor universe.

Finite control: enumerate gauges on the four-element union monoid with single-element covers given by inclusion and integer coordinates 1..3. Preserve every triple by exact comparison to its maximum context ratio. Lexicographically minimize, test pointwise minimality and exact multiplication. This toy is neither the tensor-class monoid nor a proof of countable compactness.

## IPS-01: extraction through quotient inverses

**Written lemma (all commutative rings).** Given a division-free arithmetic DAG C(x,y) of s gates, and polynomials F_1,...,F_m of total shared input circuit size S_F, construct a shared DAG with O(ms+S_F) gates for polynomials D_i such that
C(x,F)-C(x,0)=sum_i F_i D_i.
For each gate maintain its two evaluations a=a(x,F), a0=a(x,0) and the vector d satisfying a-a0=sum_i F_i d_i. At x and constant leaves d=0; at y_j use the jth unit vector. Addition and subtraction are componentwise. At multiplication of a,b use d_i = d_i(a)*b + a0*d_i(b). The ring identity proves the invariant exactly as a polynomial, even with arbitrary formal intermediate degree. Each gate adds O(m) scalar operations. If C is an IPS certificate, its two evaluations are 1 and 0, so the D_i give a Hilbert-like certificate with polynomial overhead. This is a gate-level version of standard IPS/Nullstellensatz certificate conversion.

Put J=(F_2,...,F_m), R=F[x]/J. Every refutation yields the same class [D_1]=[F_1]^-1, since units have unique inverses. Thus a sufficient condition for an unrestricted IPS lower bound is an efficiently specified family F with a size-controlled extractor E from every representative of [F_1]^-1 to a hard polynomial H (e.g. HC, or permanent outside char 2). Specify E on circuits, not expanded polynomials. If E uses at most P(s,n) gates, then lower bound L_H(n) imposes P(O(ms+S_F),n)>=L_H(n). Require exact identities over extension fields; equality as a function on the Boolean cube is weaker. An arbitrary normal-form map can expand exponentially. Hardness of one chosen certificate, even a canonical representative, does not suffice.

**Barrier lemma (fixed finite fields).** Over F_q, if g never vanishes on Boolean assignments, g^(q-2) computes the inverse function there. Binary powering uses O(log q) multiplication gates beyond g (q=2 gives 1). Thus inverse-function hardness alone cannot give unrestricted arithmetic circuit lower bounds on the Boolean cube over a fixed finite field when g itself has a small circuit. This does not produce small circuits for the coefficients witnessing g*g^(q-2)-1 in the Boolean ideal. Distinguish the quotient representative from the full proof. Over fields of other types or other quotient rings the inverse argument has different scope.

**Barrier control:** the quotient F[x,t]/(t^k) permits efficient coefficient extraction from a circuit by truncated multiplication (O(k^2 s) operations). For a unit g with nonzero scalar constant term in t and low-size coefficients, its inverse truncated series is also computable efficiently by recursive coefficients, so this particular simple truncation cannot expose a hard polynomial from a small g. More general quotient encodings need a new structural argument.

**Next gate:** first formalize the gate induction; then construct candidate polynomial-size non-Boolean systems and prove an extractor valid for all representatives, with easy inverse and syzygy adversaries. Reject if extractor needs exponential multilinearization, depends on a specific proof, or assumes the lower bound it seeks. Compare unrestricted IPS with restricted circuits separately. No unconditional IPS lower bound follows here.

## GEO-01: common-layout shortening obstruction

OpenAI's hinge uses a tensor C consisting of m-bit equality on each of D orthogonal diameters of the Euclidean unit ball. A candidate identity is a sum of A tensor networks, each with cost W (wire width times length, including external wires), with strict saving W+2D log2 A<2Dm.

**Written theorem (any field):** no strict shortening can have a single layout; nor can a sum of A networks with the same geometric edge layout and widths have strict shortening. Local tensors may vary arbitrarily. More generally, for direction u on the unit sphere and threshold a in [-1,1], let r(u,a) be m times the number of separated diameter pairs and b_j(u,a) the width of cut edges in layout j. For every cut, flattening rank of the target equals 2^r (tensor product of identity factors and nonzero rank-one factors). Fixing all internal cut bits makes each network a sum of at most 2^b_j rank-one matrices, so by rank subadditivity:
2^r <= sum_j 2^b_j <= A*2^(max_j b_j).
For a common layout this gives r<=b+log2 A. Integrate thresholds and average uniform directions. Write c_D=E|u_1|; the integral of an edge's crossing indicator is its projected length, whose directional mean is c_D times Euclidean length. The diameters give E integral r=2Dm c_D. The common layout gives E integral b=c_D W. Hence W+(2/c_D)log2 A>=2Dm. Since |u_1|>=u_1^2 and E u_1^2=1/D, c_D>=1/D, which proves the claimed obstruction. For A=1 it is exact for direct diameter wires. No tensor rank beyond ordinary bipartite flattening was used. The same inequality applies to coefficientwise/Zariski limits with the layout and widths fixed, since the rank upper bound is closed.

**Heterogeneous-layout corollary.** Let bbar be the mean across layouts and Wbar their mean cost. Every candidate obeys
E_u integral (max_j b_j-bbar) da >= c_D(2Dm-Wbar)-2 log2 A.
A strict shortening with a maximum cost W therefore requires a strictly positive cut-heterogeneity budget (also when A=1 the inequality rules the candidate out). This gives a necessary certificate statistic for search. A candidate satisfying all rank cuts may still fail tensor realizability.

**Bridge to existing VPVNP-02:** Euclidean observers on binary matrix space already satisfy the sharp bound C=2(1-2^-n) for squared full-rank versus rank-one shifts (see 02J; native status is governed by its own CI). Those observers are a restricted metric class. Anthropic's Contracting quantifies over every spectral price, and flatness asserts every finite Euclidean metric is a price. Neither result follows from the observer inequality alone.

**Search gates:** enumerate small layouts with terminals ±e_i, exact rational internal coordinates, widths, and finite field local tensors. First apply cut inequalities to eliminate impossible candidates; retain heterogeneous layouts; evaluate identities by full tensor entries (not rank only). Use squared rational lengths or rigorous rational bounds on square roots to certify strict cost; for selector overhead check the inequality with rigorous logarithm bounds. A single exact strict-shortening witness answers the OpenAI hinge for that field. A bounded negative search cannot establish the opposite case. For Anthropic's regime require a valid spectral price or a theorem transferring the shortening witness. Register held-out fields and dimensions before scaling. No resolution of the full flat/contracting question is asserted.

## LOOP-01: exact polynomial recurrent-circuit bounds

**Written theorem:** an arithmetic recurrent architecture with initialization cost s0, tied transition circuit cost s_cell per evaluation, T discrete steps and readout cost s_R gives an ordinary division-free arithmetic DAG of size <=s0+T*s_cell+s_R (with inputs/constants counted consistently and shared where possible). A cell may be nonlinear; assume the output is the specified formal polynomial. Conditional on either published lower bound for the target H, every such architecture computing H satisfies T*s_cell >= L_H-s0-s_R. The argument also applies if all cell operations are over Laurent series and the resulting formal polynomial has H as its coefficientwise limit. A real numerical accuracy guarantee does not imply border computation.

**Fast-forward theorem for tied affine state:** if h_(t+1)=A(x)h_t+b(x) with A,b polynomial circuits independent of t, width w, and fixed input x, augment the state by 1 and form B=[[A,b],[0,1]]. Then h_T is the first w coordinates of B^T*[h_0;1]. Binary powering needs O((w+1)^3 log(T+1)) arithmetic gates, plus coefficient generation, initial state, readout and an O(w^2) matrix-vector multiply. The proof is the elementary multiplication law and repeated squaring, in a commutative polynomial ring. Even exponentially many affine steps with log T polynomial cannot realize a hard formal polynomial family when width, entry generation and readout are polynomial. This sharpens the unrolling constraint for time-homogeneous affine cells. The bound is an upper bound on cost, which induces a lower-bound tradeoff under the imported hardness theorem. Parameter sharing alone makes no general claim about neural networks with softmax, normalization, arbitrary nonlinearities, switching, quantization or data-dependent halting.

**Benchmarks:** compare exact direct recurrence to augmented powering over F_2,F_3,F_5,F_7 with polynomial-valued entries and initial states, widths 1..3, steps up to 31. Compute formal coefficient arrays; do not substitute only finite-field points. For compact models, introduce a separate task where affine and nonlinear cells compete under matched width, arithmetic operation count, parameter bits, activation precision and external memory. Register a hard polynomial target and exact coefficient verification on small sizes. Report larger numerical learning results independently. Candidate transfers: Compactification-Theory for fast-forward lemma; LoopedTransformers for the tied-cell resource ledger; no new network performance is claimed.

## CROSS-01: compare the proof engines and their semantics

| Layer | Anthropic | OpenAI |
|---|---|---|
| Hinge | all Euclidean finite metrics as prices or universal contraction on binary matrix increments | existence/nonexistence of strict shortening for orthogonal diameter equality tensors |
| Direct geometry | spectral prices, flat lower bound (Paper/Flat.lean, Paper/Rate.lean) | Euclidean wire budget and varying-cut rank argument |
| Recursive side | group hierarchies, scale lifting, accelerators, replay/diagonal step | iterative shortening, ribbon switching hosts, batched replay of hypothetical circuits |
| Coefficient issue | field transfer and Laurent-series approximation | universal counting polynomial, advice and Cartier-operator treatment of limits |
| Interface | Statement.Computes, Approximates, MainTheorem, Separation | paper binary arithmetic gates, border in degree n, Standalone.vp_ne_vnp Lean endpoint |
| Claimed exponent | 4/3 | 5/4 |

The shared high-level proof architecture is a rigidity-or-compression hinge followed, in the compression regime, by self-reference with a quantified simulation budget. Treat it as a hypothesis about reusable lemmas; the exponents and hinge formulations differ. Both manuscripts require careful human semantic review; comparison above is source inspection, not independent kernel replay of OpenAI.

**Exact gate-model bridge:** Anthropic counts variables and constants as gates and uses binary + and *. OpenAI paper also permits subtraction. Over a field replace each subtraction a-b by a+(-1)*b, sharing one -1 constant. A size-s circuit translates to at most 2s+1 gates under the same input-count convention. This is a semantic bridge for exact formal computation, not an automatic equality of all definitions of VNP or the two border models. Need map family indexing, polynomial bounds, homogeneous projection and base-field extension explicitly.

**Conditional geometry bridge:** if a finite network placement admits a normalized spectral point with exponents exactly d/(2D), then spectral multiplicativity and subadditivity applied to an equality tensor covered by A networks yield 2Dm<=W+2D log2 A. Approximate exponents converge for the fixed finite placement, yielding the same inequality. Anthropic's definition of price permits arbitrary scales, so the requisite scale bound does not follow merely from the definition. Establishing this normalization or an alternative bridge is an open target. In the other direction do not identify strict shortening with Contracting absent a proof.

**Diagonal interface:** for v in any nontrivial field the equation v=v+1 is impossible. Turning that contradiction into a nonuniform lower bound needs the whole replay interface: circuit representation with arbitrary constants/degeneration, cost budget, valid self-encoding, and padding/field transfer. A finite toy with v+1 only checks the endpoint. For the next comparison, freeze common statements for circuit simulation, tensor cover, selector overhead, encoding and resource accounting; align declarations with each proof DAG; use blind reconstruction and proof-kernel checking in the DataGoblin/EvalMath gym with dependency-held-out targets.
