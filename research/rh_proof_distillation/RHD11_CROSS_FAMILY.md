# RHD-11: cross-family cyclotomic repair audit

**Stacked on MathCan RHD-10 PR #169.** The purpose of this experiment is to measure what survives when a correction policy trained at one cyclotomic field and prime is applied at another, with the target-family labels entirely withheld.

## Exact held-out mathematical family

Work over E = Q(zeta_24), p = 5. The cyclotomic polynomial is Phi_24(X) = X^8-X^4+1, and over F_5 it has four irreducible degree-two factors. The independent standard-library generator performs a simultaneous Hensel lift, reproducing the exact monic factors modulo 25:

| factor index | polynomial modulo 25 |
| --- | --- |
| 0 | X^2 + 11X + 7 |
| 1 | X^2 + 2X + 18 |
| 2 | X^2 + 23X + 18 |
| 3 | X^2 + 14X + 7 |

Their product is Phi_24 modulo 25, and their discriminants are nonsquares modulo 5. For f_j(X)=X^2+c_j X+d_j and u=1+5(a+b alpha_j), the finite first-layer norm is 1+5(2a-c_j b) modulo 25. The order-5 Dirichlet exponent is q(x)=-((x^4 mod 25)-1)/5 modulo 5, and exhaustive testing checks q(N(u))=2a-c_j b for every factor and a,b in F_5. The scalar a=3,b=0 witnesses surjectivity at every factor. These are finite-ring statements, not a construction of the full p-adic Hecke character or TyEll.

## Global-unit compatibility witness

The independent finite-ring check also evaluates the actual cyclotomic units `1-zeta_24` and `1-zeta_24^7`. Since both have global norm 1, a global finite-order character must have compatible local exponents. The first-layer logarithms give:

| factor j | log(1-zeta_24) | log(1-zeta_24^7) | determinant mod 5 |
| --- | --- | --- | ---: |
| 0 | (3,0) | (4,4) | 2 |
| 1 | (4,2) | (3,0) | 4 |
| 2 | (3,0) | (0,2) | 1 |
| 3 | (0,4) | (3,0) | 3 |

Both unit images span the first principal-unit layer at every place, and their local norm-character exponents sum to zero modulo 5 across all four places. The tests enumerate all 24 nonzero F_5-linear functionals at each factor, rejecting any single-place order-5 functional that vanishes on both units.

As in RHD-10C, the resulting **single-place ramification obstruction** is conditional on finite order, trivial infinity type, and the other places being unramified. The finite arithmetic gives the exact local unit data; the general class-field-theoretic step awaits a Lean formalization.

## Held-out episodes and policies

There are exactly 4 * 5^2 = **100** target-family root episodes, SHA-256 keyed by the selected factor and two residue coordinates. The deterministic corruption counts are:

| injected faults | episodes |
| ---: | ---: |
| 0 | 25 |
| 1 | 24 |
| 2 | 25 |
| 3 | 26 |

The zero-fault cases independently exercise a correct terminal STOP action. The ten fault kinds have the same schema as RHD-10: four local character coefficients, the global norm-sign convention, the exact conductor, unit normalization, and three missing ramified-place exceptions.

The RHD-10 frequency policy is fitted to **only Q(zeta_20), p=29 training roots**. The evaluation code exposes the held-out field parameters and redacted verifier failures. The Q(zeta_24) examples are reserved entirely for testing, including the independent factors, exact q, and expected output data. Teacher answers and injected-fault counts are unavailable to the policy.

The second policy is an explicit arithmetic transfer rule that derives local coefficients and the conductor from visible field metadata. It is a transparent **hand-written comparator**, not a trained neural model.

The evaluation measures three observation schedules:
- sparse_one_step: one visible failure, one correction budget
- sparse_recurrent: one visible failure per round, up to three corrections
- full_open_loop: all failure gates observed initially, repairs applied before one final check

Report the number of fully verified episodes, paid verifier calls, invalid and rejected edits, attempted early STOP actions, and terminal STOP/CONTINUE classification. The headline efficiency is **fully verified repairs per 100 policy-visible verifier calls**. Independent evaluator scoring checks are not charged as policy-visible calls.

## Interpretation and next proof slice

The test can expose memorization of factor-specific coefficients, the modulus and source-family action grammar. It can also show the limit of a finite-action lookup policy when the field changes. Neither policy constructs the complex automorphic eigenline U or the upstream Lean TyEll predicates.

For mathematical proof work, the pinned upstream sources still require actual HeightOneSpectrum places, a local compact open torsion-free subgroup J, surjective theta, primitive-order-ell complex chi, the adelic eigenfunction, local cuspidality and Satake matching. The different roles of the exceptional sets in SphOffU and GenTy still need a source-level bridge. The RHD-10C cyclotomic-unit obstruction excludes the simplest single-prime-support shortcut for the same order-29 finite-character class.
