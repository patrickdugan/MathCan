# NOR-37: stochastic curvature randomization and conditional exploration coverage

The prospectively registered NOR-37 experiment tests whether a positive uniform-within-atom exploration probability and a stochastic top-curvature window can preserve some of NOR-35's source-feature-aligned detection while resisting the policy-aware greedy attacker studied in NOR-36.

Each original NOR25 audit plan has a fixed eight-atom inspection quota vector t_j. New policies retain every one of those quotas, and independently choose either a fully uniform size-t_j audit subset in atom j (probability 25%, 50%, or 75%), or a curvature-weighted stochastic branch selecting h_j=ceil(t_j/2) RANDOM positions from a PUBLIC top window of size min(n_j,max(2h_j,ceil(n_j/8))) and a uniform reserve of t_j−h_j from remaining atom positions.

The exact mathematical guarantee is source- and threat-conditioned. If the blind attack fixes altered indices before the actual audit coins, and the full-uniform branch would detect that attack with positive probability, any positive mass on full-uniform exploration contributes at least that mass times the uniform detection probability. The Lean theorem formalizes the corresponding nonnegative finite weighted detection count inequality and positivity. NOR25's exact binomial reserve count continues to apply to a calibration-dependent but PRECOMMITTED attack if the subsequent reserve coins are independent, and NOR34's quota-matched uniform attack theorem continues to apply when each source/trial's eight atom inspection counts are identical. A fully realized-schedule-informed attacker still has an indistinguishable altered target.

This **does not** prove a blanket strictly positive probability when all changed coordinates lie in atoms receiving zero inspection quota, nor does it prove the empirical stochastic policy is minimax optimal. Eight greedy restarts on 256 public calibration schedules are only an approximate policy-aware attacker. The synthetic experiment uses 24 fresh RRC25 source identities and 512 independently salted held-out challenge schedules/source/arm. Honest true-target coefficient witnesses and actual hidden audit random coins are external premises; SHA-seeded public PRNG streams only support reproducibility.
