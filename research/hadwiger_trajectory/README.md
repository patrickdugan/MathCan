## HAD-01: lift symmetric rigidity to K
Let Q=[-1,1]^n, S=(K-K)/2, K a convex body. If (1-e)Q ⊂ S ⊂ (1+e)Q with 0≤e<1/3, center K at the midpoint c of its coordinate bounding box. Coordinate widths of K equal twice those of S measured from 0 in the coordinate directions. Thus K-c ⊂ (1+e)Q. For every sign vector s, choose p,q in K-c with p-q=2(1-e)s. Since s_i q_i≥-(1+e), we obtain s_i p_i≥1-3e. Given any direction u, take s_i=sign(u_i), choosing either sign at zero. Then h_(K-c)(u)≥(1-3e)||u||_1. Separation yields (1-3e)Q ⊂ K-c. Hence affine Banach–Mazur distance minus one is at most 4e/(1-3e).

Put S in John's position, with the unit ball maximal inscribed ellipsoid. By the 2025 theorem, W(S)≤(1-γ_n d_HO(S,Q))W(Q) for γ_n>0 dimension-dependent. Write δ=E h_Q-E h_S, with uniform spherical average and W=2E h. Then e=d_HO(S,Q)≤2δ/(γ_n W(Q)). Rotate to the minimizing orientation. Hausdorff support bounds imply (1-e)Q ⊂ S ⊂ (1+e)Q because h_Q≥||u||_2. In dimension 3, W(Q)=3. For e≤1/6 the BM excess is ≤8e, hence ≤16δ/(3γ_3). The dependence on δ is linear locally. This is a deduction from published symmetric stability; novelty of the elementary lifting lemma has not been established. The constant is dimension-dependent and may be too weak for numerical use.

## HAD-02: direct finite chain certificate
Let K be convex, p in K, and x_(i+1)-x_i=a_i(w_i-u_i) with u_i,w_i in K and a_i≥0. Put A=sum a_i, choose λ≥A with λ>0 and define
v=x_0-sum a_i u_i-(λ-A)p.
For every prefix j, x_j-v=sum_(i<j) a_i w_i + sum_(i≥j) a_i u_i + (λ-A)p. This is a λ-scaled convex combination; all states and all interpolated segments lie in λK+v. Each coefficient a_i can be chosen as half the gauge of the chord in S, since K-K=2S and K compact. If L_S<2, take λ=max(L_S/2,1/2)<1. Every finite ordered subset of a rectifiable curve admits this fixed λ. The compact sets x-λK have the finite intersection property (restrict to one fixed nonempty compact set), yielding a common translation for the whole curve in finite dimension. This generalizes the finite chord argument in Lemma 3.4; it is a constructive reformulation, not a novelty claim.

Sharpness: take endpoints of a diameter in the difference-body gauge (distance 2). A contracted translate cannot contain both. For arbitrary trajectory length, this is only a sufficient certificate; a backtracking path can fit in a much smaller chart.

## Exact clipped-cube control
K_t=Q_3∩{x_1+x_2+x_3≤3-t}, 0≤t≤2. Its vertices are the seven unaffected cube corners and the three points with one coordinate 1-t and others 1 (duplicates allowed at endpoints). For a direction u,
h_K(u)=||u||_1-t max(0,min_i u_i).
Reason: on the positive octant reduce the least costly coordinate by t; outside it an unaffected maximizing corner exists. Thus E h_S=E h_K and δ=t E max(0,min_i U_i), a positive linear coefficient. Also (1-t/6)Q⊂S⊂Q (choose opposite corner pairs, shortening the all-positive corner by t/3 in each coordinate when needed). S contains the Euclidean ball: h_S=||u||_1 except when all coordinates have the same sign, in which case it is ||u||_1-(t/2)min_i|u_i|. The sum of the two largest absolute coordinates dominates ||u||_2 in dimension three, since 2ab≥c² for a≥b≥c≥0. The unit ball is John's ellipsoid of Q and is inside S⊂Q, so S is exactly in John position. The centered bounding-box certificate is valid for t<2 via e=t/6; for this family the exact inner aligned cube radius of K_t is 1-t/3. No claim this equals the optimal affine BM distance.

## BPD integration protocol (proposed HAD-GYM-01)
Calibrate K on training-only activations at a fixed loop layer. Use rank 3 as a source-aligned pilot; use rank 8 and 16 only for the dimension-independent chain result. Freeze projection, convex hull and centering; report discarded residual energy separately. For polytope K=conv(V), solve per-edge LP: minimize sum p subject to V(p-q)=Δx, sum p=sum q, p,q≥0. Then a=sum p, with u=Vq/a, w=Vp/a (a=0 means zero chord). Verify witnesses and feasibility independently; exact rational or outward error bounds required for a certified claim. Online cost can be reduced by caching edge decompositions, not by changing the validation criterion. Compare greedy fixed λ segmentation with equal-length windows, Euclidean path-budget segmentation, fixed-depth, shuffled and Haar projection controls. Greedy is minimum-count among contiguous segmentations for a fixed additive budget if every edge fits, with consistent shared-endpoint convention; over-budget edges must be reported or explicitly subdivided. Measure held-out chart count, contraction margins, verifier calls, bytes, wall time, downstream steering/readout quality, and out-of-hull/projection residual errors. Geometry alone cannot establish behavioral preservation. For an affine readout f(x)=b+a·x, a chart gives f(x)≥f(v)-λ h_K(-a), so a positive certified lower bound implies sign preservation. Multiclass uses pairwise logit differences. Nonlinear readouts require separately justified Lipschitz/interval bounds. Compare matched compute and memory tiers (0/64/256/1024 MiB); split by model root and freeze validation before fitting. Do not infer high-dimensional eight-chart coverage.
