## Independent scalar implication

The actual rank proof consumes injectivity together with surjectivity of a finite-dimensional projection. In a finite-dimensional abstraction, if A:Z→U is injective and P:Z→U is surjective, dim Z=dim U. Both hypotheses matter: injection alone gives only dim Z<=dim U and surjection alone gives only dim U<=dim Z. This suggests a separate diagnostic for whether the source equality can be weakened to dim Z<=dim U for monotone positive valuation transport; B16's exact source uses equality, and such a weakening must retain the nonnegativity of its inner envelope. If eta<=R*E, 0<=R<=D0 and E>=0, then eta<=D0*E. If E<0 this implication fails: R=1,D0=2,E=-1,eta=-1. This is a conditional scalar consequence, not a replacement for the other uses of SmallLevelRankEq. Rank equality carries no global growth exponent.
