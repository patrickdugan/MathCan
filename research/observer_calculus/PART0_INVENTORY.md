## Retrofit status

For **every** row above, ROC-01 outputs a card status:
- **retrospective_partial** when only existing summaries, original 8-way random controls or some Jacobian blocks are public;
- **ready_for_replay** when a source-identical checkpoint, orthonormal U, per-state T and task, and declared H are available;
- **not_a_linear_observer** for quantized codes, symbolic quotients or isolated SAE feature responses;
- **complete** only after a full per-state card, equal-budget task-only comparator, 128 rank-matched bases and norm-matched intervention arms are evaluated.

Missing A, B, C, H or task state values appear as null with a reason. Do not backfill invented state rows or infer a causal Gram from a single task MSE. Rank-32 prospective sweep is listed separately from the <=16 historical inventory.
