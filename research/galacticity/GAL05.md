# GAL-05: Computational capital

Full paper derivations and experimental receipts:
https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/Compactification-Theory/blob/research/gal01-nonperturbative-20261128/Galacticity/GAL05.md

MathCan/GalacticityCapital.lean contains nine core-Lean candidates:
one-step minimum capital equivalence, feasible minimum, monotonicity,
composition and triple-funding inequalities, adjacent exchange in profitable,
loss and mixed cases, and monotone capacity obstruction. The sequential
monoid, sorting optimality and backward DP correctness are paper proofs
using those arithmetic certificates, not a kernel-formalized planner.
The separate hosted workflow checks the new module. No real economic or
neural-network speedup claim is made.
