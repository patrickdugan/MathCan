# Monotone certified posterior refinement under a resource ledger

Source and development audit: https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/LoopedTransformers/tree/01e40b7c0274b40bc4b9acdfa05545a35d9b24e5/experiments/logrank_bpd_18.

Condition each Boolean query on its two event sides. A cutset node v denotes a cell with exact nonnegative integer likelihood mass Z_v. Maintain an enclosure I_v containing Z_v. Refining a node with another valid enclosure uses intersection. Splitting a cell by free coordinates creates disjoint children whose masses sum to Z_v; intersect I_v with the interval [sum L_child, sum U_child]. Recursively intersect ancestor enclosures with sums of their current child enclosures. Induction gives soundness and monotonicity: every descendant refinement narrows its interval, interval sums narrow, and intersection preserves containment. For two positive event masses, P=Z_1/(Z_0+Z_1) increases in Z_1 and decreases in Z_0; the induced posterior interval narrows monotonically. The result holds regardless of how the next legal action is chosen, including adaptive choice from observed bounds. It is conditional on the likelihood/prior, and it does not prove global search optimality.

A deterministic bounded-width elimination schedule can be planned using only scopes. The table-entry count for a group of t factors whose union has scope s is t*2^s. Sum this across groups and branches to charge a complete action. Peak counted factor slots are the sum of 2^(s+1) over simultaneously live lower/upper factor tables (including the bucket while outputs are built). This ledger counts numeric table slots and join visits; it is not total process memory or bit-operation complexity. In the homogeneous integer BSC model, factor values are at most 2^n max(u,v)^m when each original likelihood factor is assigned once and each elimination sums one anchor, by expansion over boundedly many binary assignments. Use conservative integer payload sizes and explicitly account for other allocations.

LOGRANK-BPD-18: 27 worlds, 270 runs. Independent replay confirms 6244 weight bounds and budget ledgers, 821 monotone refinement actions, 437 disjoint splits and 1080 query intervals. All caps respected. Adaptive produces 140 exact query/noise instances versus 134 for fixed scope 5 and degree-cut2, but planning overhead is substantial; all 48D dense menus exceed the slot cap. Three roots per stratum, no inference of superiority or native training. Written proof, not Lean formalized.
