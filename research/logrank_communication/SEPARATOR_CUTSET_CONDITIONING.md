# Certified cutset conditioning

Source and independent finite checks: https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/LoopedTransformers/tree/369b2a79d85f7f3c9e4079cdecfdad0514791d14/experiments/logrank_bpd_17.

Let C be a subset of k binary coordinates of a uniform-prior parity likelihood model, and let x range over its 2^k assignments. Every original state is in exactly one branch h_C=x. Under that branch, each parity observation (a,y) restricts to (a outside C, y XOR parity(a intersect x)). Constant factors must remain in the likelihood product. For any event E, if the sum of likelihood weights in E and branch x is bounded by L_E(x) and U_E(x), then the unconditional event weight lies between sum_x L_E(x) and sum_x U_E(x). Apply this separately to the two target parities, and use the posterior interval L_1/(L_1+U_0) <= P(E_1 | evidence) <= U_1/(U_1+L_0), for positive denominators. A fully determined target routes all of a branch's weight to the corresponding event. Exactness follows whenever all contributing conditioned weight computations are exact.

If every conditioned factor graph has an elimination ordering whose largest scope is c (including the eliminated variable), ordinary elimination is exact with tables of at most 2^c entries each. Enumerating cuts sequentially trades as many as 2^k branch solves for reuse of bounded-size kernel tables. This is a per-table bound, not a bound on total process RAM, and no general runtime or cutset optimality claim follows. Intersecting independently valid branch bounds preserves their soundness.

LOGRANK-BPD-17: 216 runs, 648 disjoint partition cells, 5832 independently replayed kernel weights, 864 intervals, 576 exact-reference interval checks. Both public graph-selected two-coordinate cuts recover the planted hub pair in all nine structured worlds and yield exact posteriors on all 72 structured target/noise cases at scope 3. No-cut scope 5 is also exact. On 48D structured cases at noise .15, degree selection uses 550 peak counted slots versus 1488 at scope 5, with roughly twice the wall time and no observed process RSS improvement. All dense arms yield essentially vacuous intervals. The result is development-only, with three roots per stratum, no Lean formalization or native mechanistic claim.
