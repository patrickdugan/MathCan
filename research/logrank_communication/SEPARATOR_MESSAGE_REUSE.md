# Exact reusable separator messages for signed parity queries

Source: https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/LoopedTransformers/tree/1e80b84527246fc38336b00831bdcd26287e3fe8/experiments/logrank_bpd_19.

An elimination tree with the running-intersection property assigns each original factor to exactly one bag containing its scope. Removing an edge partitions the factors into two sides whose shared variables lie in the separator. The directed message is the sum of the product of factors on its side over all assignments consistent with the separator. This identity follows by induction from the message recursion, with each incoming message integrating a disjoint subtree. Calibrate both directions once.

For two variables a,b, let Z be the unsigned partition sum and Z_ab the signed partition sum after multiplying the corresponding owning-bag factors by (-1)^h_a and (-1)^h_b. Then the exact parity probability is (Z-Z_ab)/(2Z). Off-path subtrees contain neither owning bag and keep their calibrated unsigned messages. Replacing messages on the unique a-to-b path by signed contractions and inserting the second sign at b gives Z_ab by the same induction. Negative messages are valid signed sums. For a collection of queries sharing source a, compute the source-signed messages once along the union of the paths to all target owners, then finish each target contraction separately. This is exact reuse; no probabilistic independence approximation is required.
