# Noisy parity identifiability and verifier information budgets

Source: https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/LoopedTransformers/tree/b7c4754265495cd5d952d012ad9e46ef17bff076/experiments/logrank_bpd_10.

Let S be the uniform r-bit coordinate representation of the quotient of hidden signs by the kernel of the initial observation matrix. Each binary parity observation is passed through independent binary symmetric noise of rate p, with query choice allowed to depend on public metadata and past observations. Conditional on the public design (and any independent policy randomness), I(S;Y_i | Y_{<i}) <= 1-H_2(p), since conditional output entropy is at most one bit and conditional noise entropy given S and the past is H_2(p). Summing gives H(S|Y^m) >= r-mC, C=1-H_2(p). Fano yields full-state error probability >= max(0,1-(mC+1)/r). For vanishing full-state error as r grows, m/r >= (1-o(1))/C. At .15 noise C=.3901597, reciprocal 2.56305; at .35 C=.06593194, reciprocal 15.16715. Necessary asymptotic condition, not finite-sample sufficiency or a bound on arbitrary individual query errors.

Any g in the nullspace of all allowed probe rows preserves every observation likelihood. For a target outside their span, choose g that flips that target. Uniform hidden signs pair configurations of equal likelihood, proving posterior target probability 1/2 even after adaptive noisy probes restricted to that span. LOGRANK-BPD-10 independently constructed 768 such witnesses.

For a fixed posterior let Z_q=(-1)^(q dot S), mu_q=E[Z_q], rho=1-2p, and O_a the noisy bipolar observation of Z_a. Cov(Z_q,O_a)=rho(mu_(q xor a)-mu_q mu_a), Var(O_a)=1-rho^2 mu_a^2. Since a binary observation makes its conditional mean an affine function of the observation on its two-point support, law of total variance gives expected Brier improvement for binary target (1-Z_q)/2 equal to Cov(Z_q,O_a)^2/(4 Var(O_a)), or zero for deterministic O_a. Average over downstream query masks. Fast Walsh-Hadamard transform obtains all moments. This optimizes expected one-step Brier gain, not necessarily long-horizon accuracy. The adaptive 10B diagnostic did not consistently beat random probes. Written proof, no Lean compilation.
