# Exact Brier gain for a rank-expanding verifier observation

Source and independent finite audit: https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/LoopedTransformers/tree/6f4d83449f3378997a41db9c4f05b74aaca9bf23/experiments/logrank_bpd_11.

Let S have arbitrary posterior on F_2^r, and U be an independent uniform new coordinate. Query Z_q=(-1)^(U+q dot S). Observe Y=(-1)^(U+a dot S+E), where E is independent Bernoulli(p) and rho=1-2p. Conditional on S and E, Y is uniform, so conditioning on Y leaves the posterior of (S,E) unchanged. Multiplying Z_q Y cancels U, hence E[Z_q|Y]=Y rho mu_(q xor a), where mu_b=E[(-1)^(b dot S)]. A binary target (1-Z_q)/2 had prior mean 1/2; its expected Brier reduction by law of total variance is rho^2 mu_(q xor a)^2/4. In particular, one noisy observation of this kind cannot yield target confidence greater than 1-p (p<=1/2). If the new direction is absent from all observations, the target posterior stays uniform. For independent new directions observed once each, these updates factor; they do not change the old marginal posterior. These facts require a uniform independent unobserved coordinate and known likelihood; repeated or coupled new-direction observations need a different update.

This gives a task-targeted expansion score: average the squared Walsh moment at offset q xor a over public targets in the same new coset. In the 11 experiment, three rank-expanding calls made 576/576 novel target queries exact in clean conditions; at p=.35 target selection recovered 336/576 versus 315/576 with random expansion and 297/576 uniform tie guesses for within-span probes. The audit independently enumerated all enlarged-state posteriors for 12,096 saved probabilities and checked 216 target choices. No statistical superiority claim or Lean formalization.
