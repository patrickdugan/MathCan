# Marginal separator-memory value as weighted coverage

Source: https://github.com/MoralityLabAI/LoopedTransformers/tree/ee90c7f4c34573f62906d1a08a15f80ca304a53e/experiments/logrank_bpd_21.

Fix an acyclic directed message dependency graph, nonnegative local costs w_e and a multiset of direct demands d_e. For an already populated pinned cache C, a request for a cached message incurs zero counted recomputation work and otherwise incurs w_e plus recursive dependency work. Write R_e(C)=0 on C and w_e+sum_{f in K(e)}R_f(C) outside C. Expanded miss counts D_e(C)=0 on C and d_e+sum_{p:e in K(p)}D_p(C) otherwise. The total unsigned arithmetic is W(C)=sum_e d_e R_e(C)=sum_e D_e(C)w_e.

For e outside C, each expanded occurrence of e contributes an identical residual subtree of cost R_e(C). The occurrences are pairwise disjoint as rooted subtrees in the expanded request forest, since the dependency graph is acyclic (no occurrence of e can descend from another e). Caching e removes all D_e(C) such occurrences. Therefore W(C)-W(C union {e})=D_e(C)R_e(C).

Alternatively expand every fixed direct request without cache into a finite forest. Label each local-work occurrence by its message, and assign weight w_e. A work occurrence is saved precisely when a cached message label appears on the path from its request root to that occurrence (including the occurrence). The savings function is weighted set coverage, hence monotone submodular. This proves diminishing marginal savings without asserting knapsack-optimal density greedy. It assumes fixed direct demand, zero retrieval cost in the counted model, no cache fills or updates during the warm evaluation, and fixed graph. It does not prove submodularity of an online cache with admission costs or stochastic evidence updates.

For a separator tree, the dependency paths are nonbacktracking and unique between directed edges whenever reachable. Adding e to C subtracts its old D_e from unblocked descendants' D and its old R_e from unblocked ancestors' R; stop traversals at cached edges. Both quantities only decrease as the warm cache grows. Lazy heap keys therefore upper-bound current marginal value per fixed positive charged byte. Refresh stale keys, choose the feasible maximal exact ratio, break deterministic ties, and repeat. This reproduces density greedy, not globally optimal packing.

21 independently replays 18,779 selected greedy steps, 1,728 cache plans, 50,688 exact arm-query results, and 326,950 cache events. 6,601 fixture checks compare marginal equality/diminishing returns to brute expansion; 192 fixture greedy plans reproduce. At 96D/16 KiB hub-chain, marginal planning reduces arithmetic vs planned cold by 38.2% stable and 32.1% shifted; at 64 KiB warm work is zero for both current-demand plans and the cheap policy is faster. Three roots per stratum, development only. Written derivation, not Lean formalized. Next account for fill work and residence survival.
