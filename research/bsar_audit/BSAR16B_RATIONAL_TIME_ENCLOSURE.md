# BSAR-16B: exact-rational dense-path Galerkin enclosure

Parent: [BSAR-16 #213](https://github.com/patrickdugan/MathCan/issues/213). The preceding [BSAR-16A PR #214](https://github.com/patrickdugan/MathCan/pull/214) merged the exact \(t=0\) high-mode forcing audit. The reference initial data are independently pinned to BSAR-14's Gaussian-rational source at MathCan \`e2c0e1c56f6e5d2291536156709f75668d1e0f8f\` and source blob \`aa25ad74be2347acf061d631f55338802eead212\`.

**Scope:** a continuous-time a priori \(L^2\) enclosure between an explicit degree-two Gaussian-rational Fourier trajectory and the **exact finite-dimensional Galerkin ODE**, with a continuous-time high-mode residual upper bound. The arithmetic is exact rational with upward-rounded rational square-root and exponential estimates. This is an independent validated approximate trajectory, **not** a roundoff-inclusive validation of BSAR-15's NumPy FFT/RK4 trajectories. Vorticity-core certification and a PDE-derived window budget remain separate.

## Fourier conventions and finite ODE

On the flat \(2\pi\)-periodic three-torus, define the spatial-average \(L^2\) norm by

\[
\|v\|_2^2=(2\pi)^{-3}\int_{\mathbb T^3}|v(x)|^2\,dx
=\sum_k|\widehat v_k|^2.
\]

At grid size \(M=16,24,32\), the retained Fourier cube has coordinate cutoff \(K=\lfloor(M-1)/3\rfloor=5,7,10\). Let \(P_K\) select the retained cube and \(\mathbb P\) be Leray projection. The exact ODE reads

\[
v'_K=F_K(v_K):=-\nu(-\Delta)v_K-P_K\mathbb P B(v_K,v_K),
\qquad B(a,b)=(a\cdot\nabla)b.
\]

The source is real and divergence-free, with \(E(0)=2\) and \(\|v_K(0)\|_2=2\). By the exact Galerkin energy identity, \(\|v_K(t)\|_2\le2\) for all \(t\ge0\). The quadratic right side is smooth on a finite-dimensional space, so this energy bound also ensures global existence of the Galerkin ODE.

## An entirely rational time-dependent reference

The degree-two dense reference is

\[
p(t)=a_0+t a_1+t^2a_2,\qquad
a_0=v_K(0),\quad a_1=F_K(a_0),\quad
a_2=\tfrac12 DF_K(a_0)[a_1].
\]

Each coefficient is assembled through exact rational convolution, rational Leray projection, and exact rational viscosity \(\nu=1/10\). The construction retains a real, solenoidal, mean-zero Fourier polynomial supported in \(\Lambda_K\). Its full continuous-time defect is exactly

\[
d(t)=p'(t)-F_K(p(t))
=t^2 d_2+t^3d_3+t^4d_4.
\]

The high-mode forcing generated by the same reference is exactly

\[
r_p(t)=P_{>K}\mathbb P B(p(t),p(t))
=\sum_{j=0}^4 t^j r_j.
\]

The verifier recomputes both expressions through independent direct rational evaluation at registered nonzero rational times; every coefficient and conjugate pair is checked through Fourier arithmetic. The defect has zero constant and linear terms **exactly**, so the local reference error starts at cubic order. No FFT normalization, hardware rounding mode, floating-point number, or experimental convergence order enters these statements.

## Certified comparison with the exact Galerkin path

Write \(e=v_K-p\). Subtracting the equations and using that \(v_K\) is divergence-free gives

\[
\frac12\frac{d}{dt}\|e\|_2^2+
\nu\|\nabla e\|_2^2
\le\|\nabla p\|_\infty\|e\|_2^2+
\|d(t)\|_2\|e\|_2.
\]

The cancellation is \(\langle e,B(v_K,e)\rangle=0\); the remaining transport term is \(B(e,p)\). For each rational Taylor coefficient let

\[
G_j\ge\sum_{k\in\Lambda_K}|k|\,|\widehat a_{j,k}|,\quad
S_j\ge\sum_{k\in\Lambda_K}|\widehat a_{j,k}|,\quad
D_j\ge\|d_j\|_2,\quad H_j\ge\|r_j\|_2.
\]

All these upper bounds are computed using exact Gaussian rationals, the integer square root, and decimal rationals rounded **upward** on a \(10^{-9}\) grid. Let \(T=3/250=0.012\) and set

\[
X_T=\sum_{j=0}^2\frac{G_jT^{j+1}}{j+1},\qquad
Y_T=e^{X_T}\sum_{j=2}^4\frac{D_jT^{j+1}}{j+1}.
\]

Then, for every \(t\in[0,T]\),

\[
\boxed{\|v_K(t)-p(t)\|_2\le Y_T.}
\]

For a nonnegative rational \(x\), the verifier bounds \(e^x\) from above by \((1-x/m)^{-m}\), where \(m\) is an exactly selected power of two with \(x/m\le1/2\). The inequality follows from \(-\log(1-y)\ge y\) for \(0\le y<1\). This produces a finite rational upper bound, without calling a floating-point exponential.

## Spatial truncation forcing along the exact Galerkin trajectory

Define the actual full-PDE defect of \(v_K\) as

\[
r_K(t)=P_{>K}\mathbb P B(v_K(t),v_K(t)).
\]

The exact polynomial \(p\) and the comparison error give a time-uniform bound for this term. With \(m_K=(2K+1)^3-1\) and \(S_*=\sum_{j=0}^2 S_jT^j\), use the Fourier inequalities

\[
\|e\|_\infty\le\sqrt{m_K}\|e\|_2,\qquad
\|\nabla v_K\|_2\le\sqrt3 K\,\|v_K\|_2,\qquad
\|\nabla e\|_2\le\sqrt3 K\|e\|_2,
\]

and the decomposition \(B(v_K,v_K)-B(p,p)=B(e,v_K)+B(p,e)\). Since \(\|v_K\|_2\le2\),

\[
\boxed{
\sup_{0\le t\le T}\|r_K(t)\|_2
\le
\sum_{j=0}^4 H_jT^j
+\sqrt3 K(2\sqrt{m_K}+S_*)Y_T=:R_*.
}
\]

Similarly, the exact count of Fourier gradient weights gives

\[
\sum_{k\in\Lambda_K}|k|^2=K(K+1)(2K+1)^3
\]

and the certified spatial-gradient envelope

\[
\boxed{\sup_{0\le t\le T}\|\nabla v_K(t)\|_\infty
\le\sum_{j=0}^2G_jT^j+
\sqrt{K(K+1)(2K+1)^3}\,Y_T=:G_*.}
\]

The code uses rational upward bounds for each square root and for the integrals. These finite-time bounds are **a priori and independently certified for the exact Galerkin ODE**, even though their tightness must be assessed from the recorded values. The source-pinned high-mode residual at \(t=0\) is still **exactly zero** for every \(N=1\) case and for \(N=2,K=10\), and has squared \(L^2\) norm **\(1/34\)** for \(N=2,K=5\) or \(7\). The new \(R_*\) includes the nonlinear modes generated for positive time.

## Conditional full-Navier–Stokes comparison

Suppose a smooth full incompressible Navier–Stokes solution \(u\) exists on \([0,T]\) with the same initial field. The usual energy cancellation for \(u-v_K\) and the exact Galerkin residual imply

\[
\|u(T)-v_K(T)\|_2
\le \int_0^T\|r_K(s)\|_2
\exp\left(\int_s^T\|\nabla v_K(\tau)\|_\infty\,d\tau\right)ds
\le T R_*e^{TG_*}.
\]

The final exponential is also replaced by a rational upward bound. This is a **conditional \(L^2\) solution-error enclosure** assuming a smooth NSE solution throughout the chosen interval. It yields no uniform-in-frequency lifespan, global regularity result, or certified vorticity-core geometry. \(L^2\) control of velocity alone is insufficient to certify pointwise vorticity or normalized-direction half-maximum anchors.
