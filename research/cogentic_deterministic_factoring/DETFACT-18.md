## Abstract marking and segmentation

For n>1, failure of the prime definition gives a proper divisor d, n=d*q, both factors >1. The smaller factor is at most sqrt(n); the DETFACT-16 prime-divisor theorem supplies a prime p of that factor, so p divides n and p²<=n. A prime n cannot have such a prime divisor: its only prime divisor is n itself, with n²>n. For odd n, p cannot be 2. Therefore given a sound and complete base list of odd primes p with p²<=K, marking odd n<=K precisely when some such p divides n and p²<=n strikes exactly the composite odd candidates. The abstract Boolean mark predicate uses the base list directly. It is not a proof of the native first-multiple calculation. The progression n=p*(p+2t) follows from the oddness of both factors and n>=p².

Define the global odd candidates by n=3+2i, 0<=i<floor((K-1)/2) using natural-number subtraction (empty at K=0 or 1). For segment size S>0, segment j uses indices i=j*S+h with 0<=h<S and i below the global count. Euclidean division supplies the unique segment j=floor(i/S) and offset h=i mod S; the final segment is truncated. The Lean module states membership and uniqueness for these segments and proves that every segment member belongs to the global candidate list. Its label enumeration filters the global range by the abstract mark predicate and inserts 2 separately. This yields a duplicate-free list containing exactly the primes <=K given a sound and complete base list. It does not formalize equality of the concrete concatenated segmented byte-array implementation to that filter.

The maximal-exponent existence lemma uses induction on K>=1: as K increases by one, the maximal exponent either stays the same or increments when the next prime power is reached. Given a unique complete eligible label list, lift to entries (p,e) with positive maximal e. The theorem enumerated_roster_lcm combines validity, uniqueness, completeness and DETFACT-16's interval-LCM identity. It proves existence of the roster; an efficient executable maximal-exponent search and proof of base-sieve construction are separate obligations. DETFACT-17 supplies the odd-stream and split equivalence from these roster hypotheses.
