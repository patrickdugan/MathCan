# Certified interval and anytime search for monotone Horn proofs

ALG-DESCENT-11 companion, 2026-11-27. Paper proof, no Lean check. A finite public acyclic Horn graph has positive rule costs c(r), initial known set S, goal G. A legal transition adds a single new head when all premises are known. J(S) is minimum cost of a successful continuation. Strict growth makes the state graph acyclic. Under a fixed graph and additive cost, exact proved-set identity suffices for continuation value; the specific proof trace may differ. The Bellman equation is J(S)=0 if G is known, otherwise min over applicable new-head rules of c(r)+J(S union head). Acyclic ordered rules with at least one accessible alternative per head guarantee completion from initial facts.

For each fact p define hmax(p)=0 for known p, otherwise min_r [c(r)+max_{q in prem(r)} hmax(q)], with max empty=0. Define hadd analogously using sum. A topological induction on p shows hmax(p) lower-bounds the weight of every legal proof of p: take its first deriving rule, the prefix includes a proof of every premise and costs at least their maximum lower bound, then charge c(r). Extract one rule attaining hadd for each unknown fact recursively required by G; use exact fact ID to share repeated dependencies. In an acyclic graph the resulting finite selected-rule set, sorted topologically, is an executable proof. Its weight U_FF is an upper bound on J(S); it is at most hadd(G) since shared dependencies can only remove positive repeated costs from the unfolded tree. Thus
    hmax(G) <= J(S) <= U_FF <= hadd(G).
This is a constructive interval, not a learned confidence interval.

For budgeted anytime best-first search, maintain verified incumbent cost U and OPEN with exact state key, best g, reopen on improvement, and admissible bound g+hmax. Prune only paths whose admissible lower bound is >= U; retain all other unexplored paths (including the popped state at a budget stop). A learned heuristic can decide expansion order without being an admissible bound. If all outgoing transitions are enumerated, OPEN covers some prefix of every still-improving proof path: either a prefix remains unexpanded or its cheaper/equal state-equivalent prefix survives; pruned prefixes cannot improve U. Consequently L=min(U,min_OPEN(g+hmax)) <= J(initial) <= U, with empty OPEN convention +infinity. When U<=min_OPEN(g+hmax), incumbent is optimal; when L>0, U/L certifies a multiplicative bound. A finite budget may return a verified proof and a nonzero certified gap. Maintain correct predecessor paths, scope to immutable rules/initial/goal and account for memory eviction; dropping unresolved OPEN states breaks this certificate unless their lower bounds are conservatively retained. These claims concern this finite monotone task, not arbitrary proof systems.
