# Feature collisions and acceptance-aligned predictors

ALG-DESCENT-10 companion, 2026-11-27. Let a fixed candidate encoder X map proposals and a common initial history to a feature vector. Let Y be the conjunction of exact local product validity and indexed-child binding. If two equally weighted proposals have identical X and opposite labels, every predictor q(X) gives mean Bernoulli cross-entropy -1/2 log(q(1-q)) >= log 2, because q(1-q) <= 1/4. Equality holds at q=1/2. In a balanced pair a feature-only randomized decision independent of the hidden identity has expected accuracy 1/2. No model depth can remove a collision under the same input. More generally the conditional-entropy minimum of expected Bernoulli log loss is H(Y|X); extra observations may reduce it. This is a conditional statement, not a lower bound on arbitrary adaptive histories or predictors with access to the binding registry.

In a commutative polynomial monoid over a field, exchanging distinct equal-degree child polynomials preserves the parent product and the multiset of child statistics. If an encoder is invariant under the exchange while the ordered registered child identities remain fixed, the original and exchanged proposals have the same input but opposite joint acceptance. Exact product validity and any evaluation fingerprint of that product cannot distinguish the pair. Childwise binding to a trusted registry resolves the collision directly. This is an information-interface defect in the synthetic proxy, not evidence about finite-field DLP hardness.

Cache transfer lemma: in a fixed scope, if accepted child closure proves an existential proposition C(P) whose truth depends only on polynomial P, a verified C(P) may be substituted for C(Q) when P=Q. This does not establish equality of indexed witnesses, proof traces, cost-to-go, or permissions. Cache admission requires local acceptance and all required child closures. Scope must include field, registry/version and any further statement context on which C depends. A witness-indexed proposition requires a witness-preserving key. Paper proofs only, no Lean verification.
