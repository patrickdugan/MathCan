## Scheme and point

Let (P=K[z_{i,j}:(i,j)\in\mathrm{Fin}(k)\times\mathrm{Fin}(s)]) and let
(I=\langle\mathrm{copyEquation}(f,t):t\in\mathrm{Fin}(k)\times\mathrm{Fin}(r)\rangle)
be the ideal generated by the **original DEL-16 equations**, with all multiplicities
and redundant generators preserved. The coordinate algebra is (A=P/I).

A tuple (u) satisfying the original equations determines the genuine (K)-algebra
point (p_u:A\to K), built with `Ideal.Quotient.liftₐ`. The proof
`productPoint_mk` establishes (p_u([Q])=Q(u)) for **every** polynomial (Q).

The algebra structure on (K) as an (A)-module is induced by the actual point
homomorphism (p_u).

## Kernel-checked equivalences

DEL-21F defines

```lean
Deligne21F.productKahlerFiberEquiv
Deligne21F.productKahlerFiberDualEquivTangent
```

and proves (K)-linear equivalences

[
  K\otimes_A\Omega_{A/K}\;\simeq_K\;
  K^{ks}/\operatorname{span}_K\{\nabla g_t(u)\}_t
]

and

[
  \operatorname{Hom}_K(K\otimes_A\Omega_{A/K},K)
  \;\simeq_K\;\ker J_u.
]

The code uses the left-tensor orientation (K\otimes_A\Omega_{A/K}), which
is canonically the same fiber as (\Omega_{A/K}\otimes_A K). The final
tangent is DEL-20's actual derivative-defined `jacobianTangent`.

### Proof spine

1. `productKahlerPresentation` is the **genuine**
   `Module.Presentation A Ω[A/K]` from Mathlib's
   `Algebra.Presentation.differentials`. Its relation ideal is exactly (I).
2. `productKahler_fiber_right_exact` applies tensor right-exactness
   to the proved presentation; no flatness of (K) over (A) is assumed.
3. `coordinateTensorEquiv` identifies the tensor product of the
   finite free coordinate module with (K^{ks}). The coordinate formula
   evaluates each original coefficient via (p_u).
4. `differential_relation_coefficient` and
   `productKahler_relation_at_point` prove that the actual
   Kähler presentation relation has coordinates
   (u(\partial_i g_t)), using the original polynomial (g_t).
5. `productKahler_relation_map_baseChange` identifies the *whole
   tensorized relation map* with finite linear combination of those
   Jacobian rows. `productKahler_relation_range_baseChange` identifies
   its image with DEL-21D's `conormalRelations`.
6. `productKahler_fiber_kernel` identifies the kernel of the
   specialized Kähler surjection with that image. The first
   isomorphism theorem produces the desired cotangent-fiber
   equivalence, and dualizing composes it with DEL-21D.

This is a scheme-level statement. Reducing (I) to its radical
would change the module in the nonreduced examples and invalidate
the identification with the original scheme.

## Verification

- Pinned Lean 4.35 / Mathlib 4.35 kernel compilation and `#print axioms`
  of the fiber and tangent-dual equivalences.
- Expected axioms: `propext`, `Classical.choice`, `Quot.sound`.
  The proof uses neither `sorryAx` nor untrusted computation.
- Independent finite-field oracle replays the 160 DEL-21D cases:
  a smooth parabola, a node at the origin and a smooth node point,
  the double line (x^2=0), its radical mutant (x=0),
  redundant ((x^2,x^3)), two parabola product factors,
  and characteristic-two derivatives.
- DEL-21F's additional Python oracle checks vanishing of the
  *original* equations at every frozen point, the actual row
  coefficients, quotient-pairing invariance and nonreduced mutation.

## Remaining geometric work

This establishes the intrinsic scheme-theoretic tangent space at
(K)-rational points of the actual finite affine presentation, and
supplies the local tangent input for DEL-19/20. A separate
field-extension/naturality theorem is appropriate where (L/K)
geometric points are needed. The global relative incidence scheme,
proper bad-locus image closure, effective elimination degree bounds,
and Katz weak Lefschetz good-open theorem remain separate from DEL-21F.
