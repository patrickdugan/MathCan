# DEL-22B: universal original-ideal critical incidence and a rank-one determinantal chart

## Actual coefficient and source rings

Let \(K\) be a field and \(\iota=\mathrm{Coord}(k,s)\) the original
DEL-16 product-coordinate index set. The parameter ring is
\[
P=K[a_i\ (i\in\iota),c]
  =\mathrm{MvPolynomial}(\mathrm{Option}\,\iota,K)
\]
where \(\mathrm{some}\ i\) indexes \(a_i\) and
\(\mathrm{none}\) indexes \(c\). The total affine source ring is
the polynomial ring \(P[z_i:i\in\iota]\), so its formal
derivations \(\partial_{z_i}\) treat \(a_i,c\) as coefficients.

For the **original** DEL-16 lifted equation family \(g_t\), preserving
its complete ideal and all nonreduced and redundant relations,
the universal section is
\[
s(z,a,c)=B(z)+\sum_{i\in\iota}a_i z_i+c.
\]
The kernel-backed module
`Deligne04.UniversalRelativeCritical` proves that specializing the
independent parameter coefficient ring through
\(\epsilon_{a,c}:P\to K\) recovers the literal original
\(\mathrm{Deligne16.fixedSection}\). Its source-coordinate
partial derivatives commute with specialization:
\[
\epsilon_{a,c,*}(\partial_{z_i}s)
     =\partial_{z_i}s_{a,c}.
\]

## Original augmented Jacobian over the universal parameter ring

The universal augmented matrix over \(P[z]\) has row set
\(\mathrm{Option}\,\mathrm{EqCoord}(k,r)\):
\[
\mathsf J^{\mathrm{aug}}_{\varnothing,i}
    =\partial_{z_i}s,\qquad
\mathsf J^{\mathrm{aug}}_{\mathrm{some}\ t,i}
    =\partial_{z_i}g_t.
\]
Lean proves the **entire matrix equality** after parameter
specialization with the actual fixed-parameter augmented polynomial
Jacobian from DEL-22A.

For every pair of row and column selections
\(\rho,\gamma:\operatorname{Fin}(d+1)\to\) the corresponding
finite index types, define
\[
D_{\rho,\gamma}(z,a,c)
  =\det(\mathsf J^{\mathrm{aug}}[\rho,\gamma])
      \in P[z].
\]
The kernel proves the exact minor identity
\[
\epsilon_{a,c,*}(D_{\rho,\gamma})
  =D^{a,c}_{\rho,\gamma}
\]
via `RingHom.map_det`, with arbitrary selection maps,
including repeated rows and columns.

The `universalOriginalIdeal` is generated by every original
\(g_t\) over \(P[z]\). The `universalMinorIdeal` is generated
by all the size-\((d+1)\) minors, and the defined
`universalCriticalIdeal` is their sum with the universal
section ideal:
\[
J_d^{\mathrm{univ}}
    = (g_t:s\!\text{ original relations})
      + (s(z,a,c)) + (D_{\rho,\gamma}).
\]
Lean verifies that parameter evaluation maps the original
equation ideal and the universal minor ideal into their
corresponding DEL-22A fixed-parameter ideals, **preserving
their actual generators**.

## Full rank-one local converse with a nonzero pivot

Let \(L/K\) be any extension field. Choose a vector
\(r\in L^\iota\) such that the actual original-ideal
conormal fiber \(R_u=\mathrm{span}_L\{r\}\), and a coordinate
\(p\) with \(r_p\ne0\). Then the genuine cotangent
criterion has the exact reverse implication
\[
g\in R_u
  \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad
  \forall j,\quad r_p g_j-g_p r_j=0.
\]
The proof solves explicitly for the unique multiplier
\(\lambda=g_p/r_p\) using the field division identity.
Here \(g=\nabla s(u)\). The next chart theorem combines this
with DEL-22A's genuine Kähler tangent/conormal duality:
\[
\boxed{
  ds_u\vert_{T_u}=0
  \iff
  \forall j,   r_p\,\partial_j s(u)-\partial_p s(u)\,r_j=0
}
\]
with on-scheme and on-section equations carried on both sides.
A separate theorem evaluates the **actual original-polynomial
pivot minors**
\((\partial_p g_t)(\partial_j s)
 -(\partial_p s)(\partial_j g_t)\), and proves their
geometric-point vanishing is equivalent to relative
criticality whenever the chosen original row spans \(R_u\)
and its pivot is nonzero.

These chart assumptions matter. At a nodal origin or a
characteristic-two nonreduced \(x^2\) fiber, the conormal
rank can drop, so a rank-one minor chart need not describe
the same geometric critical incidence. DEL-22A's original
rank-stratum predicate remains authoritative.

## Scope of remaining DEL-22B issue #253

The **general** finite-matrix converse
\[
\operatorname{rank}(A)\le d
\iff
\text{all size-}(d+1)\text{ minors vanish}
\]
still requires a kernel-backed selection of a nonzero
\((d+1)\)-minor from \(\operatorname{rank}(A)>d\).
The work here supplies an explicit nonzero-pivot rank-one
converse and a universal ideal specialization, while that
general lemma remains open.

A complete chartwise identification with a
presentation-independent Fitting ideal of the actual Kähler
module also requires an additional kernel-backed presentation
and localization theorem. Projected-image closure, properness,
nonzero effective Bertini witness extraction, and Katz weak
Lefschetz remain independent global proof obligations.
