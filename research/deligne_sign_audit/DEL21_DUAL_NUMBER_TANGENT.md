## Infinitesimal tangent functor

Let K be a field, P=K[z_i] the actual finite multivariate polynomial ring, and I the full defining ideal generated by the DEL-16 lifted equations g_t. A K-rational point u satisfies every generator. A tangent vector v determines the first-order deformation z_i↦u_i+εv_i in the actual dual-number algebra K[ε]/(ε²).

The formal Taylor identity at first order is

    Q(u+εv) = Q(u) + ε ∑_i eval_u(pderiv_i Q) v_i.

DEL-21A constructs a genuine ring homomorphism named firstOrderEvaluation from P into Mathlib DualNumber K, using the real MvPolynomial partial derivatives, and kernel-proves its multiplication law. The firstOrderEvaluation_eq_zero_iff theorem proves zero evaluation precisely when both the ordinary value and the directional derivative vanish.

The theorem firstOrder_ideal_iff_generators proves annihilation of the chosen ideal generators is equivalent to annihilation of every polynomial in their ideal span, over every characteristic. There is no replacement by the set-theoretic zero locus or radical.

The theorem firstOrder_factors_through_quotient_iff constructs an actual ring homomorphism

    (P/I) →+* DualNumber K

whose composition with the quotient map is firstOrderEvaluation exactly when the ideal is contained in its kernel. This is the functor-of-points definition of affine first-order deformations into dual numbers, retaining nonreduced structure.

The theorem product_jacobian_tangent_iff_firstOrder_lift identifies these quotient-ring dual-number lifts for the actual DEL-16 lifted ideal of U^k with membership in DEL-20's pderiv-defined jacobianTangent subspace. The base point is explicitly required to satisfy the defining equations.

## Independent exact verification

The 120-case standard-library Python regression suite covers F2, F3, F5, F7, F11, F13 and F17. It independently implements sparse bivariate polynomial evaluation, formal directional derivatives, dual-number multiplication, multiplication by arbitrary test polynomials, and first-order ideal vanishing. Frozen categories are the parabola y=x², the node xy=0, the nonreduced double line x²=0, redundant generators (x²,x³), and a radical-replacement mutant.

At x=0 the quotient K[x,y]/(x²) admits the infinitesimal x-direction v_x=1 because ε²=0; K[x,y]/(x) rejects this direction. Both schemes have the same ordinary K-rational zero set. The node xy=0 has an enlarged tangent at its intersection, and these differences remain apparent over characteristic two.
