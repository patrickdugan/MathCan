# OLM-13: Sudoku orbit separation and the factorization ambiguity of SAE self-selection

November 15, 2026. Mathematical continuation of OLM-12's exact decoder self-selection cone. Experimental companion: SMC-13 in Compactification-Theory, which trains independent 44K recurrent Sudoku checkpoints and fits fresh SAEs on newly generated Sudoku families.

## 1. A provable independent solution family

For a completely filled 9×9 Sudoku grid \(G\) define the number of *intercalates*, alternating two-digit rectangles,

\[
I(G)=\sum_{r<s}\sum_{c<d}
 [G_{rc}=G_{sd}]\,[G_{rd}=G_{sc}]\,[G_{rc}\ne G_{rd}].
\]

**OLM-13A (orbit obstruction).** The integer \(I(G)\) is invariant under any bijection of the nine digit labels, any permutation of the nine row indices, any permutation of the nine column indices, and matrix transposition. Consequently it is invariant under the smaller Sudoku-preserving group generated by row swaps within bands, band permutations, column swaps within stacks, stack permutations, digit relabeling and transposition.

**Proof.** A digit bijection preserves equality and inequality of cell values. Row/column permutations biject the enumerated unordered pairs of rows and columns, so every alternating rectangle corresponds to exactly one in the transformed grid. Transposition swaps the roles of row and column pairs and retains the same condition. QED.

The canonical cyclic Sudoku grid used by the source generator is

\[
B_{rc}=1+\big(3(r\bmod3)+\lfloor r/3\rfloor+c\big)\bmod9.
\]

**OLM-13B (canonical zero).** \(I(B)=0\). An exhaustive integer loop over its 36 unordered row pairs and 36 column pairs certifies the claim, yielding 1,296 candidate rectangles. Every Sudoku produced by the upstream source full-grid generator has \(I=0\), because that generator only performs the transformations named above. Therefore any completely solved grid with \(I>0\) is **provably outside that entire canonical source orbit**.

The new applied generator samples valid Sudoku solutions with randomized backtracking after filling three diagonal boxes, then accepts only those with positive \(I\). It retains disjoint source seeds for training, validation, feature alignment and holdout evaluation. A unique puzzle generated from one such solution need not have the same orbit invariant as its partial clues; the invariant applies to the *full target solution*, while puzzle uniqueness ensures those clues specify it.

The invariant distinguishes solution templates without claiming the new family is distributed uniformly among all Sudoku grids.

## 2. Invariance of internal SAE cross-talk under representation gauges

Let an overcomplete linear SAE have encoder \(W\in\mathbb R^{m\times d}\), decoder \(D\in\mathbb R^{d\times m}\), and internal encoder-decoder cross-talk \(K=WD\). OLM-12's all-amplitude self-selection for an atom \(t\) uses

\[
K_{tt}\ge0,\qquad K_{tt}\ge K_{jt} \quad(\forall j\ne t).
\]

The strict version uses positive, strict inequalities and determines a finite per-dictionary cone count \(R(K)\).

**OLM-13C (gauge-invariant count).** Let \(A\in GL(d)\) be a hidden-state change of basis and \(P\) an m×m permutation matrix. Define \(W'=PWA^{-1}\), \(D'=ADP^\top\). Then

\[
K'=W'D'=PKP^\top,\qquad R(K')=R(K).
\]

**Proof.** Multiply the matrices and cancel \(A^{-1}A\). Simultaneous permutation of the rows and columns only renames the tth diagonal and its competing entries, preserving every column-dominance truth value and therefore its count. QED.

Thus the **number** of strict self-selecting atoms is an appropriate basis- and feature-label-independent cross-checkpoint summary. Matching named neurons across two unrelated checkpoints remains an additional inference task; neither this invariant nor equal values of R supplies that alignment. The physical *angular margin* uses a specified Euclidean metric and is invariant under orthogonal hidden changes of basis, while general invertible A need not preserve Euclidean angles.

## 3. Perfect linear reconstruction can coexist with different cone counts

**OLM-13D (factorization nonidentifiability).** Exact input reconstruction \(DW=I_d\) alone places no fixed value on the strict self-selection count \(R(WD)\). Already at d=1, m=2, consider

\[
W_0=\begin{pmatrix}1\\1\end{pmatrix},\quad
D_0=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\end{pmatrix},\quad
W_1=\begin{pmatrix}1\\-1\end{pmatrix},\quad
D_1=\begin{pmatrix}\tfrac12&-\tfrac12\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Both satisfy \(D_iW_i=(1)\). Their cross-talk matrices are

\[
K_0=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
K_1=\tfrac12\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\-1&1\end{pmatrix}.
\]

For K0, the first column ties its diagonal with the competitor and the second has zero diagonal, so **R(K0)=0**. Both columns of K1 have strictly positive diagonal exceeding the other entry, so **R(K1)=2**. QED.

This counterexample concerns unconstrained linear autoencoders. The actual SMC-13 model has ReLU and hard top-four sparsity, so it does not transfer the exact-reconstruction construction to those nonlinear training dynamics. The logical lesson is narrower: a large R is an **internal factorization property**, and a claim about task-specific reasoning semantics requires separate data, causal or generalization evidence.

## 4. Calibration-only alignment and heldout evaluation

For two SAEs that receive paired external inputs on a calibration cohort, let \(X\in\{0,1\}^{N\times m_A}\) and \(Y\in\{0,1\}^{N\times m_B}\) record positive selected feature occupancy at corresponding tokens. Define the centered coactivation matrix

\[
C_{ij}=\sum_{n=1}^N(X_{ni}-\bar X_i)(Y_{nj}-\bar Y_j).
\]

For equal feature counts m, a permutation P maximizing \(\sum_i C_{i,P(i)}\) provides a **data-derived alignment**. Its score on fresh heldout paired inputs is the observed mean fraction of positively active top-k matches under P. Training an alignment only on calibration pairs and freezing it before heldout evaluation prevents direct test-label leakage into that permutation.

**OLM-13E (null covariance).** If the two binary features X_i and Y_j are independent Bernoulli variables across sampled paired inputs, then \(\mathbb E[C_{ij}]=(N-1)\operatorname{Cov}(X_i,Y_j)=0\). For dependent data, centering subtracts the product-marginal contribution and measures only within-pair coactivation association.

**Proof.** Expand \(\sum X_nY_n-N\bar X\bar Y\). Under iid sampling its expected value is \(N\mathbb E[XY]-\mathbb E[(\sum X)(\sum Y)]/N=(N-1)(\mathbb E[XY]-\mathbb E[X]\mathbb E[Y])\). QED.

An optimal assignment can still overfit finite calibration data. The experimental comparison therefore reports heldout accuracy and a calibration-input permutation null, preserving learned SAE weights and all feature marginals.

## 5. Scientific registration

SMC-13 preregisters independent teacher training seeds, unique novel Sudoku solutions with strictly positive intercalate counts, and separate train, validation, alignment and heldout puzzle-ID groups. The independent teachers share the original 44K recurrence architecture but are initialized from scratch, making the architecture controlled and the weights independent. Both the original pretrained teacher and new teachers will be evaluated on novel and canonical puzzle families with missing-cell accuracy.

The key falsification is whether trained self-selection is also common in a matched iid Gaussian top-four SAE. Preliminary two-seed local prototypes produced 84/96 and 82/96 strict cones for SAEs on random recurrent hidden states and 96/96 and 92/96 on Gaussian activations. These are exploratory pre-registration observations; new CI receipts must replace them for final comparative claims.

All cone counts remain conditional on a neuron being strictly selected at an anchor. The new puzzle family and checkpoint do not automatically grant semantic feature labels, causal necessity, or transferable Sudoku reasoning. No Lean kernel proof or general priority over known combinatorial invariants/representation identifiability arguments is claimed.
