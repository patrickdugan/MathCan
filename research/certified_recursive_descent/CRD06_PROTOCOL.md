## Finite latent-state law
Independent hidden increments X_t uniform in {-1,+1}. Start Z_0=+1, Z_t=Z_(t-1)*X_t. Independently sample reliability r_t from {.90,.98} uniformly. Observe Y_t=X_t times a sign noise independent across t, with P(noise=+1|r_t)=(1+r_t)/2. Input is (Y_t, (r_t-.94)/.04). Binary terminal label 1{Z_L=+1}. For L>=2 any single (Y_t,r_t) is independent of Z_L; observing all transitions gives exact posterior p=(1+product(Y_t*r_t))/2. Any proper subset of transition observations is independent of Z_L conditional on sequence length and reliability. Uniform hidden increments are crucial. The synthetic chains are independent; no planted success condition. Verify the information identities by exact sign enumeration at L=2..8 and report empirical observed single-symbol and full-parity accuracy on test. Bayesian filter is exact for this generator (prior and channel known); no claim about actual factorization.
