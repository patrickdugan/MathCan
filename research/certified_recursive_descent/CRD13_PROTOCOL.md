## Exact representation
Six latent states with integer prior weights 1..12 (only states 0,1,2 feasible), k=1 or 2 terminal probes after all sensors. Each sensor has feasible-state predicate mask in 0..7 and integer reliability a/d with d>0, d/2<=a<=d. Infeasible-state predicates arbitrary, irrelevant after expectation denominator cancellation. Work in exact BigInt numerators. Canonicalize a mask by complement if smaller (flip observation), discard constant masks and a/d=1/2, reduce rational accuracy by gcd, group identical (mask,a,d) keys. A group of n identical sensors has n+1 count outcomes: multiplicity binomial(n,h), feasible masses multiply by a^h*(d-a)^(n-h) or its reversal. Group order fixed descending n, then descending reliability, then mask/denominator/numerator. Count contributions as BigInt; use denominator total prior weight times product of retained sensor denominators to powers n. Exact grouped value = sum top-k mass over group count outcomes; constant/uninformative sensors integrate out. No claim of polynomial complexity for arbitrary sensor alphabets.

## Certified pruning
At grouped prefix w, remaining denominator D_s, lower bound L=TopSum_k(w)*D_s; upper U=sum(w)*D_s. If current chosen top-k subset S satisfies w_i*product min(a,d-a)^n >= w_j*product max(a,d-a)^n for every i in S,j outside S, products only over remaining groups where predicates differ, the same subset is optimal for every completion. Then its entire contribution is exactly L. Handle zero weights/reliability one by integer inequality, no divisions. This sufficient condition may be loose; failure triggers expansion, not a false certificate. Precompute pairwise suffix factors. Exact DFS with pruning, compare to unpruned grouped DFS. Best-first bounded variant maintains disjoint prefix partition and exact integer sums of lower/upper bounds, expanding largest gap (ties insertion order) for at most 32/128/512 internal expansions; report generated nodes, heap peak, exact interval widths and nestedness. Audit bound covers exact reference wherever feasible; large unique-reliability cases without exact reference rely on proven enclosure, not empirical correctness. Also check exact value monotonic in k and [0,total feasible mass].
