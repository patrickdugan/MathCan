## Exact local formula
Remaining m mandatory sensors, b calls, k=min(3,b-m). If k<=0 value zero. If m=0 choose top k feasible posterior masses. If k>=3 value total feasible posterior mass. Otherwise enumerate 2^m future sensor-bit words. For each word u and feasible state j in {0,1,2}, compute unnormalized mass p_j Pr(u|j). Sum largest k masses and sum over words. Infeasible states need not enter the formula because posterior denominators cancel in the expectation. Forced reveal Q=V (only local legal action); a forced legal probe j has Q=p_j+sum largest min(b-1,2) other feasible probabilities.
