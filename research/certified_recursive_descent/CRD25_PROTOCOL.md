## Storage and modes
Direct (unbounded frozen scalar), Scalar (frozen CRD-24 bounded scalar), Tree (frozen CRD-24 64 vector records) as trace controls. PackedFold caches leaves/root support and folds sequentially, PackedTree caches balanced segment supports, SplitTree same tree but separates leaf/deadline from product regions, Sibling computes base product excluding indices changed from expansion parent, reuses it for outcomes with same changed-index set and merges the changed leaf factors; product region cleared at beginning of each expansion group and not used outside it. Outside expansion use plain packed fold without product caching. SiblingJoint same with joint leaf likelihood sweep. SplitTree and Sibling separate leaf/deadline/product so product eviction cannot evict leaf values. Packed modes use variable-size packed FIFO circular pair arena: Float64 cost and failure interleaved, 2 slots per support entry, reserve one pair for empty support to bound metadata count. Complete records carry computed horizon and length; smaller horizon truncates on read, larger must recompute. Atomic insertion precharges coefficient writes and victim removals, evict oldest physical records until enough free capacity, stale superseded records may remain until FIFO removal (charge evictions). Copy supports out before reuse; charge read/write coefficients, any truncation scans, lookups, segment indices and scratch initialization via existing meter. For identity no new coefficient copy needed during pure merge. Split shares total pair capacity equally between leaf/deadline and product store.
