## Mathematical policy
At any observed state s, alive branch i has local optimal success V_i(t) if given at most t actions with no interbranch interruption. Independent hidden states/sensor noises conditional on observed local evidence. For integer allocation t_i>=0, sum t_i<=b, sequentially execute locally optimal policies with their assigned caps, do not redistribute unused actions. Visit already-active assigned branches before unopened assigned branches; when opening at full cap, abandon an active branch that has zero remaining allocation. Since finished assigned branches or initially unassigned branches can be evicted and cap>=1, a static plan is legal under the CRD-11 irrevocable abandonment rule. Static objective F(s,b)=max [1-product_i(1-V_i(t_i))]; dynamic program minimizes product of failures over integer allocations, O(n b^2) candidate combinations. Retain witness allocations. This is a lower bound on full adaptive optimum, not an upper bound. For V intervals [L,U], optimize L and U separately; their maxima enclose the best static objective. Selected lower allocation is certified optimal only if its value lower bound dominates all other allocation upper bounds (or bounds meet with exact tie); otherwise report regret bound U*-L_selected. No independence guarantee for correlated candidate trees.

One-step receding action score H(s,a,b)=P(success immediately)+sum_{nonterminal outcomes} P(o|s,a) F(s_o,b-1). Use exact local CRD-12 formula with memoization and DP allocation; no global optimal lookahead in scorer. Choose max H with deterministic higher action ID ties (score quantization 1e-12). A static optimal plan's first legal action has H>=F(s,b), because after action the remaining static caps can still be used, including forced local first probe of an optimal static plan. Dropped/empty branches have value zero; unused actions can be discarded. Thus max H>=F(s,b) and the receding policy's value >=F(s,b) by backward induction, under the stated assumptions. Check inequality on every evaluated receding decision state. Two-step variant H2 uses max H at each nonterminal outcome before applying F; count extra computation separately. Do not infer two-step dominance over one-step policy (lookahead depth changes choices). Static and receding policies use observed state only, independent finite law and public sensors. Exact means finite model evaluation in floating arithmetic here, not formal outward rounding.
