## Matched resampling theorem

Retain the QBTC-05 model: N iid uniform labels in [M], independent row encodings conditional on labels, and k independent-of-table uniformly selected distinct destination positions. Require 1<=k<=N/2 and write L=N-k for the number of untouched rows. Instead of choosing k source positions with replacement from the untouched rows, choose a uniform ordered k-tuple of DISTINCT untouched positions. Copy their labels to destinations, independently redraw each destination's encoding from its conditional law, and keep source identities hidden from the observer (the observer can know the destination set, old and new table). Let S_match,k, S_copy,k, S_ind,k be expected costs in [0,B] for these matched, with-replacement and independent marginal experiments.

Conditional on the old table and destinations, with-replacement source indices are uniform on [L]^k. Distinctness has probability (L)_k/L^k independent of the old table. Conditioning on this event gives the matched experiment. Hence its probability measure is pointwise dominated by L^k/(L)_k times the with-replacement measure; this persists after fresh conditional encoding and forgetting source indices. For label tuple y with multiplicities m_v, and untouched counts c_v, the exact matched label probability is product_v (c_v)_(m_v)/(L)_k. The with-replacement numerator is product_v c_v^m_v over L^k. This gives the same domination directly.

On the QBTC-05 good histogram event, the with-replacement conditional law is dominated by (1+delta)^k times the independent marginal law. On the bad event bound the matched cost directly by B (do not amplify the bad-event bound). Thus for 0<delta<=1,
S_match,k <= [L^k/(L)_k]*(1+delta)^k*S_ind,k + B M exp(-delta^2 L/(3M)).
Also the matched and with-replacement replacement laws have total variation at most 1-(L)_k/L^k <= k(k-1)/(2L), by coupling with the with-replacement sample and redrawing a conditional distinct tuple on failure. This TV statement concerns these two experiments, not independent labels. For Boolean disagreement or squared Euclidean change the independent k-row bound from QBTC-05 supplies S_ind,k<=k*S_ind,1. With delta=1/k and polynomial k in lambda, N=2^(2lambda), M=2^lambda, the additional factor L^k/(L)_k tends to one and the histogram exception is negligible. The transfer requires actual product row laws and fresh conditional encodings. It does not cover adaptive sources, sharing a single source, copying physical bits, revealing source identities as additional input, or the correlated KQT inverse table/PH overlay.

## New extraction barrier: hidden planted matching

Let E be a uniformly random matching of k unordered disjoint edges on N vertices, k<=N/2. Conditional on E, sample a uniformly random H:[N]->[M] with equal labels at endpoints of each edge in E (M^(N-k) compatible tables). Equivalently choose uniformly oriented distinct destination-source pairs then copy each source label into its matched destination, starting with iid uniform labels. Each unoriented matching has the same number 2^k k! of oriented ordered representations. The final distribution of H given E is uniform over compatible tables. Side information may be sampled from a law depending only on the final table H, not on E conditional on H; in particular independently freshly encoded rows conditional on H are allowed.

Given a table h, the posterior distribution of E is uniform among all compatible size-k matchings. Write c_v for the occupancy of label v. For a chosen colliding edge e in bucket v with c_v>=2, all unordered edges in that bucket have equal conditional inclusion probability by within-bucket permutation symmetry. If J_v counts planted edges lying inside the bucket, then
Pr[e in E | H=h] = E[J_v | H=h]/binom(c_v,2)
<= min(1/(c_v-1), k/binom(c_v,2)).
The first bound uses J_v<=floor(c_v/2), the second J_v<=k. It applies to an arbitrary randomized selector with output a pair or failure, even with access to the whole decoded table, provided the side-information condition holds. For k=1 the QBTC-04 exact identity recovers the sharper average probability M/binom(N,2) if N>M and the selector always finds a collision.

Concentration: in the oriented representation there are L=N-k untouched iid uniform labels, whose occupancy lower-bounds every final bucket occupancy. For 0<delta<1, put t=ceil((1-delta)L/M), and assume t>=2. The event min_v c_v<t has probability at most M exp(-delta^2 L/(2M)) (lower-tail multiplicative Chernoff and union bound). Therefore
Pr[selector returns a planted edge] <= min(1/(t-1), k/binom(t,2)) + M exp(-delta^2 L/(2M)).
Cap at one when necessary. At N=2^(2lambda), M=2^lambda and fixed delta=1/2, this bound is negligible EVEN IF k=N/2 (the first term is O(M/N)); for polynomial k the second option is O(k M^2/N^2). Planting a dense hidden matching therefore does not make arbitrary-collision extraction efficient merely by increasing the number of planted edges. This is an information-theoretic barrier to a particular extraction plan, not a collision-finding lower bound. No claim is made if the full modified oracle leaks E conditional on H; any such correlation must be modeled explicitly.

Exact posterior counting: for bucket occupancy c, the number of internal matchings of size j is (c)_(2j)/(2^j j!). Multiply these bucket polynomials in an indeterminate z; the coefficient of z^k counts compatible matchings. The optimal table-only selector chooses an edge with maximum compatible-matching incidence. The aggregate optimum is sum_h max_e incidence(h,e) divided by (number of matchings)*M^(N-k). This finite optimum is not the efficiency or security of a quantum adversary.

## An extraction-relevant stability lower bound

Let a valid-only selector A(h;r) output a collision or failure, and let q be its success on a random table. Choose k distinct uniform destination rows independently of H and the selector coins r; refresh them with independent uniform labels to form H'. Reuse the selector coins r in both evaluations. Conditional on A(H;r)=e being a collision, the probability that the destination subset hits its endpoints is
u_k = 1-binom(N-2,k)/binom(N,k).
Conditional on hitting one or both endpoints, the new pair is unequal with probability 1-1/M. Thus the original valid edge is invalidated with probability exactly q*u_k*(1-1/M). Valid-only outputs must differ on that event, so
Pr[A(H;r)!=A(H';r)] >= q*u_k*(1-1/M).
For one-hot Euclidean output including failure, the corresponding squared-distance lower bound is twice this quantity. This is a decoded-table independent-refresh calculation. For polynomial k<<N, u_k is only O(k/N); the QBTC-05 upper bound alone does not give a contradiction for a high-success selector. In fact lexicographic first collision is a constant-depth polynomial-size-in-N circuit on explicit decoded labels and always succeeds on a compressing table. The remaining security proof must exploit hidden encodings and coherent oracle access, not the mere existence of a low-sensitivity selector.

## Prospective Bitcoin route: target-before-key binding

A weaker alternative to full collision resistance may be useful for a ledger with an agreed ordering or finalized epoch. Song (arXiv:1409.2187, Sec. 5.1) lifts the OWF-to-hash-tree-signature construction to post-quantum security, including its universal one-way hash family. KQT (arXiv:2411.02554v1, Theorem 1) explicitly invokes that signature reduction for its quantum-computable OWF. Audit the UOWHF subconstruction in this same oracle, including coherent implementation, quantifiers and key sizes, before claiming a concrete target-collision-resistant interface. Classical OWF implication alone does not establish unrestricted CR.

A possible QBTC-07 design: publish and agree on a full canonical batch as the target, then sample an independent unpredictable hash key, and retain (key, digest) as a later reference to the fixed batch. Under the appropriate quantum TCR game, a later alternate opening is a target collision. The protocol requires an authenticated finalized target before key selection, no adversarial key choice, a key-grinding bound, data availability and explicit reorg semantics. If the batch must already be committed by a short binding hash, the argument is circular. For an arbitrary permissionless transaction builder selecting both messages after a global hash key, TCR does not imply binding. This would be a delayed checkpoint/archival model, not a direct replacement of Bitcoin txids or a consensus protocol. A raw full-history commitment/order baseline from QBTC-04 can isolate these assumptions. Full CR remains necessary for the original general ledger interface unless a different protocol supplies sufficient binding.

Sources: https://arxiv.org/html/2411.02554v1 (KQT), https://arxiv.org/abs/1409.2187 (Song), https://www.cs.umd.edu/~jkatz/papers/rompel.pdf (classical OWF-to-UOWHF proof; not itself a quantum reduction). These sources motivate the designs; the finite matching and stability lemmas above are our written derivations and not claims of novelty or independent formal certification.

## Exact local pilot

qbtc06_check.mjs enumerates N in {4,6,8}, M in {2,3}, k=1..N/2. It checks 3,935 untouched-label contexts, 29,841 ordered conditional label outcomes, falling-factorial counts against explicit distinct source tuples, and three good-histogram thresholds. For 18 matched-plant configurations and 29,841 table/configuration instances, it enumerates matchings directly, independently counts them with occupancy polynomials, checks posterior incidence bounds and optimal table-only selector success, and checks both conditional bounds at min occupancy thresholds 2,3,4. Twelve independent-refresh cases enumerate all tables, destinations and replacement labels for k=1,2. Exact integers are within the safe range for these finite cases. The fresh-encoding extension follows by the written mixture argument; this pilot checks the matched label laws, not arbitrary physical encodings. Example N=8,M=2: best planted-edge recovery k=1: 1/14, k=2: 11/56, k=3: 43/112, k=4: 9/14. These small-instance optima are consistent with the asymptotic bound; the occupancy condition is weak at small N. For N=8,M=2,k=2 the lexicographic selector invalidation probability is 13/56 and output-change probability is 2395/7168. All local checks passed. Independent CI has not run for this stage.
