# RH-COMP certificate foundations

MathCan/RHCompCertificates.lean is an initial core-Lean proof candidate, included in the default MathCan target on this branch. Run lake env lean MathCan/RHCompCertificates.lean and lake build; inspect the printed axioms. No Lean compiler was available during preparation, so compilation is pending. No sorry or new axiom is intended.

Scope: signed integer single-use factoring identities, sound matched-bound minimization, joint feasibility at independent sink optima, future-word equivalence and congruence refinement, arithmetic interval dual bound under explicit charge hypotheses, and a rank-growth horizon under its stated premise.

Remaining: instantiate future equivalence with actual GF(2) linear row spaces; prove rank-nullity/cardinality and strict stabilization; formalize interval charging and latest-end exchange without assuming the aggregate inequality; formalize DAG replay-to-state-graph correspondence; prove exact root-cap threshold over finite admissible helper sets. These are not claimed as finished kernel proofs.
